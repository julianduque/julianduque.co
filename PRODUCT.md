# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who meet Julián through a talk, a podcast, a community, or a Heroku article and want to know who he is, what he works on, and where to find his content: conference organizers, developers, community members, and colleagues. They arrive from a link, scan quickly, and leave with one or two follow-ups.

## Product Purpose

julianduque.co is Julián Duque's personal site: a single reference for his profile, talks, communities, open source, interviews, books, professional Developer Advocate work, and the "Node.js En Vivo" post archive. Success means a visitor finds the thing they came for in seconds and understands what Julián does without reading a biography.

## Positioning

A working index, not a portfolio pitch. The site links out to the real artifacts (YouTube, GitHub, Heroku blog, podcasts) and keeps its own chrome quiet.

## Operating Context

- Static site generated by Eleventy 3 from `content/`, styled with plain CSS in `assets/css/`, small vanilla JS bundled by Rollup.
- Lists are curated JSON under `content/_data/` (talks, communities, opensource, interviews, books, workPosts, workVideos, workPodcasts).
- Posts live under `content/content/YYYY/` as Markdown with `title`, `date`, `category`, and `tags`; most embed a YouTube iframe.
- Deployed as static output from `_site/`; RSS at `/blog.rss`, sitemap, robots, 404.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: `/` (intro + index), `/about/`, `/work/`, `/content/` (paginated posts), `/archive/`, individual posts, `/404.html`.
- Site search was removed in the 2026 redesign; the archive is the way to find posts.
- YouTube thumbnails are derived from item URLs at build time (`youtubeThumbnail` filter); no local thumbnails exist.
- Google Analytics tag is configured in `content/_data/siteconfig.cjs`.
- Dark and light themes must both ship; the visitor's choice persists in `localStorage.theme`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Julián Duque. Tagline: "AI, Developer Relations, Education, and Community".
- Visual world is shared with the Tarot Arcana project (`../tarot-arcana/DESIGN.md`): IBM Plex Mono as the single voice, midnight ink and dawn paper palettes, muted violet as the only accent, hairlines instead of containers, no gradients or glass.
- Social presence: GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, YouTube, RSS.

## Product Principles

1. Index first: every list is scannable without opening anything.
2. Link out honestly; the artifact lives where it was published.
3. One voice, one accent, one ground.
4. Same reading order at every width.
5. Keep the site cheap to maintain: JSON lists and Markdown posts, no build-time cleverness.
